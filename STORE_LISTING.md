## Full description (4,000 characters max)
Inside Jokes turns your group trips into a trivia game only your friends can win.

Upload photos from the trip, put one on each card, and ask the questions no stranger could answer. Who took this photo? Whose plate is this? Who disappeared within an hour of this one? Pass the phone around, draw a card, and find out who actually pays attention.

BUILT FROM YOUR PHOTOS
• Make a deck for any trip, and pick the place from a list of cities and countries
• Add your photos and turn each one into a question card
• Mystery zoom: start zoomed in on one spot, then reveal the whole photo
• The background shifts to match the colors of each photo

NO WRITING REQUIRED
• Pick from a library of ready-made questions: Classic, Food, Night out and Story
• Type the friend who's the answer. The other choices are filled in with your crew's names
• "Who took these?" mode turns a whole album into cards in seconds
• Optional 18+ category for drinking stories, hangovers and bad decisions

PLAY FOR SOMETHING
• Give every player an inside-joke title
• Win stars stack on your name: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Legend
• Loser's mystery photo: someone secretly picks a photo, and last place gets it, plus a dare
• Winner's secret question: the winner asks the group anything, and everyone answers anonymously

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your decks and photos stay on your phone. Share a deck with a code you can text to friends.

One phone. Your crew. All the stuff you swore you'd never bring up again.
