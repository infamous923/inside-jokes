Inside Jokes Privacy Policy

Last updated October 31, 2026

Inside Jokes is a trivia game you play with friends on one phone. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. This page explains what happens to the things you put into it.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create stays on your device: decks, questions, answers, player names and titles, win stars, Venmo usernames, Partiful links, guest lists, and the photos you add. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that stores your data, so we can't see it, sell it or share it.

Photos you upload are shrunk and saved in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

What you choose to share

When you share a deck, the app creates a code that contains the deck's name and questions (not photos). That code goes wherever you send it, like a text or group chat, and it's covered by the privacy terms of that app.

Links to other services

If you tap a Venmo or Partiful link, it opens that service outside Inside Jokes. Their own privacy policies apply there. Inside Jokes never handles payments.

Fonts

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time it opens. Like any web request, this lets Google see your device's IP address. Google describes how it handles this in the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Age

Inside Jokes includes an optional 18+ question category about drinking and nightlife. The app is intended for adults.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions can be posted on the project's GitHub issues page.
